Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

RC Agents ("we", "our", "us") operates the rcagent.space website and provides AI-powered commerce automation services for Royal Chaussures. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your personal data when you interact with our services via Messenger, WhatsApp, Instagram, or our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, profile picture, and social media handles when you message us on Messenger, WhatsApp, or Instagram.
  • Conversation Data: The content of messages you send to our AI agents, including text, images (e.g., photos of shoes), and order-related queries.
  • Order Information: Purchase history, shipping address, phone number, and payment status when you place an order through our Shopify store or via chat.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage patterns when you visit our website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide AI-powered customer support and sales assistance across messaging platforms
  • Process and fulfill your orders, including coordination with ZR Express for shipping
  • Improve our AI agents' responses and recommendation accuracy
  • Send order updates, shipping confirmations, and promotional offers (with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data with:

  • Shopify: For order processing and inventory management
  • ZR Express: For shipping and delivery coordination
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook/WhatsApp/Instagram): As required by their platform APIs to deliver messages
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (Railway), AI model providers (DeepInfra), and analytics services

5. Data Retention

We retain your conversation data for up to 12 months to improve our AI models and provide continuity in customer support. Order data is retained for 5 years for accounting and legal purposes. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at royalchaussures2@gmail.com.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS 1.3), HMAC-SHA256 signature verification for webhooks, and access control mechanisms. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Material changes will be notified via our messaging platforms.
